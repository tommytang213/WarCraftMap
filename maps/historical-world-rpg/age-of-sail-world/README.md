# Age of Sail: The World

A very long single-player global historical sandbox/RPG for Warcraft III.

## Working scope

- English-language game
- roughly 1450-1820 historical progression
- Europe, Africa, Asia, the Americas and Pacific
- countries/polities with internal provinces/states and settlements
- capturable cities
- evolving technology, institutions, economies and military units
- exploration, trade, quests, treasures, companions, armies and fleets
- origin/current allegiance government rewards
- peerage, land grants and eventual territorial rule
- national/country-specific units and content
- effectively uncapped strategic ownership; active Warcraft objects are performance-managed
- very long campaign with robust versioned persistence

## Player-development contract

The player is expected to play near-final/full builds, not repeatedly test individual development features. Automated and developer-side validation is part of the project design.
